Use the **Agents** page to see which AI agents are registered in an `aidb` database, what they're allowed to access, and how often governance denies their actions. Select **Agents** in the cluster sidebar to open it. The page is available only for `aidb` databases; HM and Loki sources don't record agent identities.

The page has two tabs: **Roster** and **Behavior**.

## Browsing the roster

The **Roster** tab lists the agents registered in `aidb`, five per page, with their **Agent** name, **Purpose**, **Groups** (the sessions, connections, or traces they took part in), and **Last Active** time. Use **Search...** to find an agent.

Selecting a row opens **Agent Details** on the right, with the agent's role, declared purpose, registration date, its **Permissions**, and links to its recent activity. The permissions come from the Postgres grants on the agent's role, for example `SELECT (email, name) on public.customers` or `EXECUTE on 3 functions in billing`.

Select **View Full Details** to open the agent's page, which adds:

- **Granted Access Permissions** — Every permission, with its scope.
- **Recent Activity** — The agent's latest sessions, with their start time, duration, and decisions. Select an identifier to open it.

The viewer connects read-only, so **Edit Granted Permissions** and **Suspend Agent** are shown but disabled. Change an agent's permissions in the database.

If the viewer's role can't read the `aidb` agent catalog, the tab shows **Agent registry unavailable**.

## Reviewing agent behavior

The **Behavior** tab shows, for each agent, its governed executions and denied decisions over the last 30 days. It's a first look at which agents keep being denied, not a verdict.

The cards at the top count **Agents observed**, **Governed executions**, **Denied executions**, and agents that **Needs attention**. The table lists each agent's **Groups**, **Executions**, **Denied** count, **Denied rate**, **Denied per day**, **Last denied** time, and a **Signal**:

| Signal              | Meaning, with the default thresholds                                   |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **No decisions**    | No allowed or denied decision was recorded for the agent.              |
| **Not enough data** | Fewer than 10 governed executions, too few to judge.                   |
| **OK**              | Less than 5% of executions denied.                                     |
| **Watch**           | From 5% to less than 20% denied.                                       |
| **Repeated**        | 20% or more denied.                                                    |

The table is sorted by signal, so the agents denied most often come first. The **Needs attention** card counts agents with **Repeated**. A signal says what happened, not why: a high denial rate can mean an agent asks for more than its purpose allows, or that the grants for its purpose are too narrow for legitimate work. Check the denied steps before acting.

Expand a row to see the agent's most common **Denial reasons**, and follow the link to its sessions in **Activity**. A note below the table shows the thresholds in use.

If the chart value `bff.governanceDecisionsEnabled` is `false`, the table shows only **Agent**, **Groups**, and **Spans**, without decision columns or signals.
