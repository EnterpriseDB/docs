Manually deploy and configure EDB Postgres Distributed (PGD) according to your chosen [deployment pattern](/pgd/current/planning/deployment-patterns/). The pattern determines which nodes and groups you deploy.

For a learning or test environment, use the [Quickstart guide](/pgd/latest/quickstart).

To deploy a PGD cluster manually:

1. Meet the [prerequisites](prerequisites) for your pattern.
1. [Install the database and PGD software](installing-database-and-pgd) on each host.
1. [Configure the cluster](configuring-cluster).
1. [Access your cluster](accessing-cluster) and verify replication.
1. Optionally, [install the extensions](extensions) you need.
