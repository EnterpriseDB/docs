# Checking your cloud readiness

When using Your Cloud Account, each time you create a cluster, you must ensure the readiness of your cloud to work with EDB Postgres AI.

- [Preparing your AWS account](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/edb-postgres-ai/cloud-service/getting_started/your_cloud_account/preparing_cloud_account/preparing_aws/): Prepare your AWS account to manage databases on EDB Postgres AI Cloud Service.
- [Preparing your Azure account](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/edb-postgres-ai/cloud-service/getting_started/your_cloud_account/preparing_cloud_account/preparing_azure/): Prepare your Azure account to manage databases on EDB Postgres AI Cloud Service.
- [Preparing your Google Cloud account](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/edb-postgres-ai/cloud-service/getting_started/your_cloud_account/preparing_cloud_account/preparing_gcp/): Prepare your Google Cloud account to manage databases on EDB Postgres AI Cloud Service.
