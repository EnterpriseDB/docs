# Getting started using EDB container registries with Kubernetes

To pull operator or operand images from EDB container registries, you need to:

1. [Get your token](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/repos/getting_started/get_your_token/).
2. [Choose your registry](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/repos/getting_started/with_k8s/choose_your_registry/).
3. [Deploy operators](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/repos/getting_started/with_k8s/deploy_operators/).

> **Tip: Next steps**
>
> Once you've designed your system and are preparing to go to production, you'll want to configure a local registry
> to provide access to EDB images, custom images, and system or third-party images. This is always a good idea for
> performance and reliability, but if your requirements include air-gapped installation or sovereignty-compliance,
> then a local, private registry is an essential part of the system.

- [Choose a registry](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/repos/getting_started/with_k8s/choose_your_registry/): Overview of available EDB container registries and their purposes.
- [Deploy operators](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/repos/getting_started/with_k8s/deploy_operators/): Network prerequisites and a directory of specific guides for deploying EDB operators.
