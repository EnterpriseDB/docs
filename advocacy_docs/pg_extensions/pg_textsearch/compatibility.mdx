## Supported platforms

- RHEL, Rocky, and Alma (EL) 8 on amd64 and ppc64le
- RHEL, Rocky, and Alma (EL) 9 on amd64, arm64, and ppc64le
- SLES 15 SP7 on amd64 and ppc64le
- Debian 12 (Bookworm) on amd64 and arm64
- Ubuntu 22.04 LTS (Jammy) on amd64
- Ubuntu 24.04 LTS (Noble) on amd64

## Not currently supported

- s390x
- Debian versions earlier than 12
- PostgreSQL versions earlier than 17

## Supported PostgreSQL versions

- EDB Postgres Advanced Server 17 and 18
- EDB Postgres Extended Server 17 and 18
- PostgreSQL 17 and 18
