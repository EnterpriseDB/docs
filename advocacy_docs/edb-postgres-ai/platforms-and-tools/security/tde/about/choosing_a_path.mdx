---
title: Choosing a path
description: Find the right page for your TDE scenario, whether you're creating a new cluster, upgrading, or changing Postgres distributions.
---

TDE encrypts data as it's written, so you can only enable it when you create a database cluster — you can't turn it on for data that already exists. Which page you need depends on whether you already have a cluster, and if so, what you want to do with it.

| Your scenario                                                  | Source database server                                                        | Target database server                                                                          | See                                                                        |
|------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| You don't have a cluster yet                                     | None (new cluster)                                                               | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) or Enterprise Postgres                             | [Creating a new TDE-enabled cluster on self-managed infrastructure](../self-managed/) |
| Perform a minor upgrade and add encryption                       | Unencrypted Enterprise Postgres (a given major version, earlier minor version)   | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (same major version, later minor version)                             | [Adding encryption or upgrading with pg_upgrade](../upgrading/)             |
| Perform a major upgrade and add encryption                       | Unencrypted Enterprise Postgres (a given major version)                         | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (a later major version)                                               | [Adding encryption or upgrading with pg_upgrade](../upgrading/)             |
| Perform a major upgrade on an encrypted database                 | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) (a given major version)        | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) (a later major version)                           | [Adding encryption or upgrading with pg_upgrade](../upgrading/)             |
| Maintain the Postgres distribution and add encryption            | Unencrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) (a given major version)      | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) (same major version)                              | [Adding encryption or upgrading with pg_upgrade](../upgrading/)             |
| Maintain the Postgres distribution and rotate encryption keys    | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) (a given major version)        | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) (same major version) with new encryption keys     | [Adding encryption or upgrading with pg_upgrade](../upgrading/)             |
| Change the Postgres distribution and add encryption              | Unencrypted PostgreSQL (a given major version)                                   | Encrypted Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) or Enterprise Postgres (same major version)        | [Migrating from PostgreSQL to Enterprise Postgres (Oracle Compatible) or Enterprise Postgres](../postgres_to_extended/) |

If you're choosing how to protect the data encryption key rather than which page to follow, see [Protecting the data encryption key](how/#protecting-the-data-encryption-key).
