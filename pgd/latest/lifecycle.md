# Maintaining your PGD cluster

EDB Postgres Distributed (PGD) provides a suite of tools and methodologies for maintaining the health of your multi-master clusters, keeping performance tuned and your data protected through regular testing and backups.

- [Testing and tuning for performance](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/testing-tuning/) — Guidance on benchmarking your distributed workloads using `pgd-bench` and optimizing system parameters for maximum throughput.

- [Backing up and restoring](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/backup-restore/) — Best practices for securing your data, including physical and logical backup strategies tailored for distributed multi-master topologies.

- [Monitoring](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/monitoring/) — Tracking cluster health through Postgres Enterprise Manager, SQL, and PGD Monitor's built-in web UI, REST API, and Prometheus endpoint.

* [Testing and tuning](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/testing-tuning/): Learn how to test and tune EDB Postgres Distributed clusters.
* [Backing up and restoring](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/backup-restore/): Back up and restore a PGD cluster using physical backups or pg\_dump.
* [Monitoring](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/monitoring/): Monitoring EDB Postgres Distributed through Postgres Enterprise Manager, SQL, and PGD Monitor
* [Migrating to PGD](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/latest/lifecycle/migrating/): Migrate from PSR to EDB Postgres Distributed.
