# Managing lifecycle operations

EDB Postgres Distributed (PGD) provides a suite of tools and methodologies for maintaining the health and evolution of your multi-master clusters. Managing lifecycle operations effectively ensures that your high-availability environment remains performant and secure through software updates, migrations, and configuration tuning.

- [Deploying and configuring](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/6.2/lifecycle/deploying/) — Instructions for provisioning PGD clusters across different environments, including manual installations, TPA-automated deployments, CloudNativePG deployments, and Hybrid Manager integrations.

- [Upgrading PGD](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/6.2/lifecycle/upgrades/) — Detailed workflows for performing minor and major version upgrades for both the underlying PostgreSQL engine and the PGD extension with minimal downtime.

- [Testing and tuning for performance](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/6.2/lifecycle/testing-tuning/) — Guidance on benchmarking your distributed workloads using `pgd-bench` and optimizing system parameters for maximum throughput.

- [Performing backup and recovery](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/pgd/6.2/lifecycle/backup-restore/) — Best practices for securing your data, including physical and logical backup strategies tailored for distributed multi-master topologies.
