# PXF Upgrade and Migration

When you upgrade your WarehousePG cluster, you must perform some additional steps in order to bring your PXF configuration up to date.

_WarehousePG 6.x to 6.newer upgrade_: You may be required to perform PXF upgrade actions when you [upgrade from a previous WarehousePG 6.x version](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/warehousepg/6/pxf/upgrade_pxf_6x/).
