Monitor data protection across your WarehousePG (WHPG) cluster.

- [Securing backups:](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/supported-open-source/warehousepg/wem/protecting-data/backups/) Manage the status and history of backup operations to ensure reliable recovery points.
