# Settings

LiveCompare settings are defined in the `.ini` files that you use to configure LiveCompare. The settings are divided into three categories: general settings, connection settings, and filter settings.

- [General](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/livecompare/3/settings/general/): All the general settings available for LiveCompare 3.0
- [Connections](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/livecompare/3/settings/connections/): Available connection settings in LiveCompare 3.0
- [Filters](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/livecompare/3/settings/filters/): How to filter tables, rows, columns, and conflicts in LiveCompare.
